It started with a table

Beautiful things
begin with meaning.

EverAfter began around a long dinner table in 2012, with a conviction that the most memorable celebrations are never about having more. They are about choosing better — a flower with a provenance, a courtyard with a point of view, a quiet pause before the music begins.

Today, our studio designs weddings and celebrations across India and wherever your loved ones gather. Our team is intentionally boutique, accepting only a limited number of commissions each season so every couple receives our dedicated artistry and presence.
